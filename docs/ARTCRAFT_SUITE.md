# ArtCraft Crafting Apps

The curated list is based on the official ArtCraft Team site
https://getartcraft.com/apps and its individual project repositories
under https://github.com/storytold.

Included (17): PhotoCraft, VectorCraft, FilmCraft, LightCraft, PdfCraft,
EffectCraft, AniCraft, DesignCraft, FigCraft, TextureCraft, MaterialCraft,
WordCraft, SoundCraft, DeckCraft, CADCraft, GridCraft, Craft Anything.

ArtCraft Studio is a **separate** AI creative product; the official
ArtCraft Launcher is a separate package manager, not an editor.

Icons are copied unmodified from the official `craft-launcher` app-icons
directory, with the applicable MIT notice in
[third_party/ARTCRAFT-ICONS.md](../third_party/ARTCRAFT-ICONS.md).
This independent hub is not endorsed by ArtCraft.
